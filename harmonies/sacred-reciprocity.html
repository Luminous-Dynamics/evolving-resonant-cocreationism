Sacred Reciprocity

Love as Generous Flow

The dynamic flow of giving and receiving that characterizes all healthy relationships. Trust replaces transaction; abundance flows to where it's needed, creating spirals of generosity rather than cycles of extraction.

Sacred Reciprocity isn't a ledger balanced to zero — it's a relationship where both parties are actually better off for having been in it. Extraction can look like exchange right up until you ask who's left depleted at the end. This harmony asks that question honestly, of every relationship, including the ones with your own tools and institutions.
