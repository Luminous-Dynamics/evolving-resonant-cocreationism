Universal Interconnectedness

Love as Fundamental Unity

The unifying field underlying all diversity, fostering deep empathy and shared being. Everything is connected in the web of being — what affects one affects all. Technology at its best reflects and strengthens these sacred connections, rather than isolating each of us into a separately-monetized silo.

This harmony is the honest answer to "externalities aren't my problem." They are — an externality is just a connection someone decided not to look at. Universal Interconnectedness insists that the boundary of a system's responsibility is wherever its effects actually reach, not wherever its accounting stops.
