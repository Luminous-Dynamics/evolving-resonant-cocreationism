Love as Harmonious Integration — The Sacred Alignment

The drive toward integration, balance, and luminous coherence — creating profound order, boundless creativity, and deep peace. Resonant Coherence isn't imposed order — it's the order that emerges when every part of a system, or a life, is allowed to actually listen to every other part. A codebase, a relationship, a nervous system: each has its own version of this harmony, and each fails the same way when it's absent — through fragmentation, static, parts working against each other instead of with.
