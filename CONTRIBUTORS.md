## How to Be Listed

Contributors are added when they:
- Submit merged pull requests
- Report significant bugs
- Improve documentation
- Provide design feedback
- Test and validate features
- Share and promote the philosophy

### 🌟 **Philosophy & Content**
Contributors who enhance the philosophical framework, writing, or content.

### 💻 **Code & Technical**

### 📚 **Documentation**
