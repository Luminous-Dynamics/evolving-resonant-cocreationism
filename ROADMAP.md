## 💜 Consciousness-First Development Principles

As we evolve, every feature must align with:

1. **Accessibility First**: No feature launches without full accessibility
2. **Privacy by Design**: User data is sacred, never exploited
3. **Performance Matters**: Respect users' time and devices
4. **Sustainability**: Minimal environmental impact in hosting/design
5. **Open by Default**: Code, processes, and decisions are transparent
6. **Co-Creation Always**: Community input shapes direction
7. **Quality Over Speed**: Launch when excellent, not just "done"
